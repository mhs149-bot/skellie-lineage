Generations are numbered forward from Alexander (Gen 1) to Arthur (Gen 10). This is our family chart numbering, not SAR reverse numbering. Years not yet documented are marked dates TBD rather than guessed.

    c. 1735 est. – 1816

    From Scotland (Gurock / “Guras,” near Glasgow) to Cambridge, Washington County, NY in 1774. Revolutionary soldier; town officer by 1776; church elder 1785. Buried Old Turnpike Graveyard.

Inspiration

🎖️

Ebenezer Skellie (1842–1898)

Civil War Medal of Honor, 112th New York Infantry, Battle of Chaffin’s Farm. Ebenezer is on a side branch, not the SAR patriot line above. He is part of the same bloodline’s story and an inspiration for the family, not a separate paperwork line.

Optional naming note: cousin James Arthur Mathews (Jim) is another “Arthur” in the wider family.
