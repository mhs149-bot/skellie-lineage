The line

Generations use SAR numbering: Gen 1 is the applicant (Michael), counting up the paternal line to Gen 9, the patriot (Alexander). Display order is you → ancestor. Every date links to its source (small numbers); nothing is guessed. Anything not yet proven says TBD.

What is proven so far:
  • Gen 3–9 dates are verified (Harrison Roy through Alexander) from gravestones, the NY State Death Index, the Social Security Death Index, and cemetery records.
  • Links Gen 9→8→7 are well documented (Alexander→John→Edward: 1911 church history; John & Hannah's family Bible).
  • Links Gen 7→3 (Edward through Harrison Roy) rest on Find a Grave family links and volunteer census notes. They are consistent, but census pages or death certificates are still needed for an SAR packet.
  • Gen 3→1 is family knowledge (living people; not searched in records).

  9. Gen9

    Alexander Skellie

    Strong (death) · birth year derived · SAR Gen 9 (patriot)

    b. c. 1735, Scotland
    d. 25 Mar 1816, Cambridge, Washington Co., NY1,5,3

    The original stone reads “died March 25, 1816, in the 81 year of his age. Revolutionary Soldier.” A modern marker reads “16 Albany County Militia, 1735–1816.”1

    Militia roster lists “Skelly, Alexander.” Town officer by 1776; church elder 1785; buried Old Turnpike Graveyard.2,3

    Conflicts: born 1735 vs 1736. Origin is “Guras”/Gurock near Glasgow per family papers, but one online tree ties him to a 1759 baptism in Sorbie, Wigtownshire. That match is unverified, so the Scottish origin is treated as family tradition.4

Where sources disagree

  • Alexander's birth year: 1735 (veteran marker, Find a Grave) vs 1736 (one online-tree note). His original stone says he died “in the 81 year of his age,” which fits either.
  • Alexander's origin: “Guras”/Gurock near Glasgow (family papers) vs a 1759 baptism in Sorbie, Wigtownshire cited on one online tree. That match is unproven.
  • John's birthday: 21 Apr 1764 (Find a Grave, WikiTree) vs 10 Apr 1764 (family Bible, written after 1850).
  • George's middle initial: “P.” on the gravestone vs “D.” in the 1942 state death index. Same place (Mina), age 72, and his wife May died there in 1940, so it is almost surely him. The certificate will settle it.

Inspiration

🎖️

Ebenezer Skellie (1842–1898)

Civil War Medal of Honor, Co. D, 112th New York Infantry, Chaffin’s Farm, 29 Sep 1864. Ebenezer was the son of James Skellie, Edward’s brother, which makes him a first cousin of Job. He is on a side branch, not the SAR patriot line above. He is part of the same bloodline’s story and an inspiration for the family, not a separate paperwork line.22,10,11

Optional naming note: cousin James Arthur Mathews (Jim) is another “Arthur” in the wider family. Harrison Roy’s wife was Mary D. (Mathews) Skellie.
